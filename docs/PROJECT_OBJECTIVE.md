# Project Objective

Build a website for an AI agency / consulting firm that can launch, present, and sell multiple AI-based solutions and products.

## Core Positioning

The website should represent a flexible AI solutions company, not a single-product business. It should support consulting services, custom AI development, business automation, and multiple domain-agnostic or industry-specific AI products over time.

## Business Goals

- Sell AI consulting services to businesses.
- Showcase AI automation and workflow solutions.
- Launch multiple AI products under one agency brand.
- Support solutions across many domains and industries.
- Build trust with prospects through clear offers, use cases, and contact paths.

## Product Direction

The platform should be domain-agnostic, meaning it can support products and solutions for many industries such as healthcare, education, real estate, finance, ecommerce, HR, legal, logistics, and others.

## Suggested Website Structure

- Homepage with clear positioning, featured solutions, featured products, industries served, and strong calls to action.
- Services page or section for consulting, automation, AI assistants, workflow optimization, analytics, integrations, and custom AI apps.
- Products catalog where new AI products can be added over time.
- Industry/use-case sections to show broad applicability.
- Case studies or example use cases.
- About section explaining the agency vision and approach.
- Contact or booking flow for consultations, demos, and project inquiries.

## Example Positioning Lines

- We build AI-powered products, automations, and custom business solutions across industries.
- Launch AI into your business with custom products, automation systems, and intelligent workflows.
